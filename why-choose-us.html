Who we are · Est. 1991

An operator first. A platform by design.

Operating in the logistics industry since 1991, AGC Global Logistics is a Dubai-based land transport company rebuilt around one idea: road freight in the GCC is fragmented, opaque and manual — and it doesn't have to be.

Three decades of on-the-ground experience, now combined with our own fleet, a growing network of verified partner carriers, and a single digital operations platform. Structure, transparency and control — for every load, on every lane.

Our vision

To be the GCC's most reliable land freight network — where every truck, lane and load is visible, verified and on time.

Our mission

To organize and digitize fragmented road freight, giving shippers cost control and carriers consistent, fairly-paid work.

How we operate

Asset-backed where it matters, asset-light where it scales — with a 24/7 operations desk behind every shipment.

Our philosophy

Better freight starts with the person behind the wheel

A better supply chain starts with education. Every driver in the AGC network — our own team and our partner carriers — is vetted, documented, and put through continuous training and upskilling.

By turning owner-operators into true industry professionals, we directly enhance everything we deliver to you: the more professional our drivers become, the better the service, safety and efficiency on every single load.

Green logistics

Greener lanes for a better planet

We strongly believe in making this planet a better place to live. In road freight, the biggest environmental win is eliminating waste — empty return trips, poor routing, idle time.

That's where we attack first: matching return loads, optimizing every route, and building per-trip carbon visibility into our platform — so your business meets its sustainability goals without sacrificing speed.
